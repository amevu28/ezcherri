WELCOME TO CHERRI CRAFT!!
This short guide follows the same sequence as the tutorial video.

WHAT YOU WILL NEED <333

Foam sheets
Scissors
A ruler and pen
Glue dots or glue stick
Paper straws
Pipe cleaners

THE BASIC STEP GUIDE

1. Cut a 12.5 cm foam square.

2. Fold it diagonally to find the center point.

3. Make a small hole in the middle.

4. Cut slits from each corner toward the center.

5. Add holes on alternating corners for folding.

6. Fold corners into the center and secure with glue.

7. Add foam washers to help it spin smoothly.

8. Wrap pipe cleaner around straw for structure.

9. Stack everything in order so it rotates properly.

10. Lock everything in place so it doesn’t come apart.

YOU CAN DO IT <333
