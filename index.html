WELCOME TO CHERRI CRAFT!!
Today’s activity is making these really fun, handheld, toddler-safe windmills. They’re simple to make, don’t require many materials, and are a great little craft project to do together ~

WHAT YOU WILL NEED <333

Foam sheets
Scissors
A ruler and pen
Glue dots or glue stick
Paper straws
Pipe cleaners

THE BASIC STEP GUIDE

1. Cut a 12.5 cm foam square.

2. Fold diagonally both ways to find the center.

3. Make a small hole in the center.

4. Cut slits halfway from each corner toward the middle.

5. Add holes on every other corner.

6. Fold the corners with holes into the center and glue them down.

7. Cut two small foam circles with center holes to use as washers.

8. Wrap a pipe cleaner around a paper straw.

9. Add one foam washer, thread the windmill through, then add the second washer.

10. Trim and curl the pipe cleaner end to secure everything.

YOU CAN DO IT <333
